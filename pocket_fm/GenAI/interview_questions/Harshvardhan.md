# Interview Questions
**Company:** Pocket FM
**Profile:** GenAI

**Result:** Accepted.

Ensure that you are comfortable implementing topics from ML, ML Lab and
preferably AI and AI Lab cold - i.e. you can write code for decision trees,
linear regression, logistic regression, k-means clustering, maybe boosting and
bagging. (I was asked for k-means clustering and another candidate was asked for
random forest without AI help; I could refer to Google though.)

Read the "Attention Is All You Need" paper. Be comfortable with Query, Key and
Value matrices, how to get them, why to use them, self-attention scores,
multi-head attention, masked multi-head attention, and encoder-decoder vs
decoder-only tasks. (Go through the first 10 lectures of Prof. Mitesh Khapra's
LLM NPTEL lecture series.)

General advice: divert the discussion to your projects, always link to some skill
that you used in something you did, as the interviewer only knows as much as you
tell them about yourself.
