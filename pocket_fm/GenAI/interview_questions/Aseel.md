# Interview Questions
**Company:** Pocket FM
**Profile:** GenAI

**Result:** Accepted.

The interview was online. Be thorough with the fundamental derivation of all ML
algorithms, know all the loss functions and have thorough knowledge of why we are
using that specific loss function.

Also come up with easier analogies for explaining your projects before the
interview - it makes explaining much easier. Explain how your project is
different and the pros and cons of the underlying architecture used. Sometimes
they may want to go really deep into your project, and if it was a group project
be thorough with what work your groupmates did.

Apart from the projects it was just explaining different ML algorithms.
