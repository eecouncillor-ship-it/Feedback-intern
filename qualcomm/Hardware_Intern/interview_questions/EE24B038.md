# Interview Questions
**Company:** Qualcomm
**Profile:** Hardware Intern 

For Qualcomm : They asked resume projects and a parity puzzle.
For TI : They asked resume projects but in much more depth and also did not provide much guidance for answering the questions. They also asked some simple STA problems, asked to implement a half adder with 2:1 Muxes.
For Nvidia : They started off with CMOS logic, asked me to implement a 3 input NAND gate in CMOS, then they asked what the static power loss is and why it occurs. After that they gave a plot (time period vs voltage) of the response of a chip where green indicated the chip passed and red indicated the chip failed, and asked why it failed at a certain region. Then they asked some STA questions and asked about resume projects.
