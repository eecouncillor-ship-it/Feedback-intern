# Interview Questions

**Profile:** Quantitative Research

**Result:** Rejected.

**General observation:** Focused more on ML and stats (especially the normal

**Interview 1**
- Two fair dice are rolled. Given that the product is even, what is the probability
  that the sum is odd?
- From a large population with a 70% success rate, 10 datapoints are sampled and
  only 6 are successful. A colleague offers you a 1:1 bet - if the actual success
  rate is less than 70% you lose your money, and if it is >= 70% you double your
  money. Should you take the bet?
- What are Lasso and Ridge regression? What are their use cases? Give scenarios
  where one method is more appropriate than the other.
- What is PCA? Say you run a PCA on stock price data - what do you think the first
  two components represent?
- What is multicollinearity?

**Interview 3**
- Say you are given a strategy (over the past year) with Sharpe ratio = 1 (assume
  Sharpe ratio = mean / std dev). Find the probability that it will make a loss in
  the next year.
- Given three vectors X1, X2 and Y (values given), find the regression
  coefficients. (X1 = k * X2, so multicollinearity is observed.)
- Find the expected maximum value of 1000 normal random variables.
- Open ended: given stock price data, try to come up with strategies to make money
  (statistical / ML based, not hypothesis based).
