# Interview Questions
**Company:** Millenium 
**Profile:** SWE

There were 3 technical rounds with 3 different people, and one HR round. In first round he asked me to explain something that I have been working on, I told him about one of my projects on my resume, then he started asking me some basic DSA related questions like how would you maintain median on a stream of data in o(1), tell code for verifying if given number is a power of 2 or not, etc.. then he asked me some basic probability questions like expected flips for HTH pattern, since I answered everything correctly he asked me some hard regression question, I told him i hadnt studied regression that deeply then he told lite only and then asked me about AI, how did you use AI, do you know how AI works and stuff like that. The second round was more math based i was asked a lot of basic probability questions like Bayes theorem, basic expectations and simple and common puzzles, and at the end she asked me to write pseudo code for implementing LFU cache, then also asked me how i would implement TTL for the same. The third round was very short, he looked at my resume, I had an order book matching implementation, he then asked me what is an orderbook, how is it represented, then asked me to show him an example of how order gets filled and that was it. The HR round was also very short, she asked me for a basic introduction, then asked me if I had any other SLs, then asked me which would you prefer and just made me a soft offer, it wasnt that much of an interview.
