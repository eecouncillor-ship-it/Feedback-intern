# Interview Questions
**Company:** Samsung Korea
**Profile:** Associate Engineer

The interview had one case study question. The scenario was making a newsletter summarizer model for Samsung's ecosystem. Along with the explanation of the problem, there were around 8 questions. One was about how to convert strings into a useful data input. The next few questions were along the lines of what model you would use, its advantages and disadvantages, what would you do for overfitting, and how would you protect people's privacy if this was made into an app
