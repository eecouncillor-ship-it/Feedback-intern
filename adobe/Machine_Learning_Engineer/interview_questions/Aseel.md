# Interview Questions
**Company:** Adobe
**Profile:** Machine Learning Engineer

**Result:** Accepted.

The interview is split into 40 minutes of ML knowledge and projects, and a final
20 minutes of solving a medium-level DSA problem.

In the initial 40 minutes you have to introduce yourself and the interviewer asks
you to pick two projects you have done. If it is a large project try to shorten
it - focus on the part you have done and use analogies; it helps the interviewer
and also demonstrates your level of understanding. I felt the machine learning
round was a walkthrough of the ML-1 course; be thorough with all the derivations
taken in class, you do not need to refer to anything else. If your projects
contain deep learning or anything outside the curriculum, be thorough with
everything you implemented - in my case I was asked for a walkthrough of the
transformer architecture as my project had it.

For the DSA round they asked a very easy array problem, but I heard others were
asked medium-level graph traversal questions, so be prepared for that.
