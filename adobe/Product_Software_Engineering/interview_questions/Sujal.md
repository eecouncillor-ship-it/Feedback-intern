**Profile:** Product Software Engineering

**Result:** Offered (rejected the offer). Only 1 round.

- DSA: given a PDF of words, how do you find the top k words in the PDF? Second

  words is not fixed, the PDF keeps growing)?

  things written in it. (The interviewer was very interested in C++17.)

  smart pointers, etc.
- One fun question: which Adobe product have you used and why?
