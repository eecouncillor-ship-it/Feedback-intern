# Interview Questions
**Company:** Adobe
**Profile:** Product(SDE)

1- Round (1 hour)
1st half of interview is Resume based, they will discuss your projects in depth so its very important to have an in depth knowledge about your resume.
2nd half is DSA, so in this they will ask DSA questions related to graph, DP, sliding window, Sorting, stack and queue. Its important to solve some sheet like striver as questions mostly revolve around it.
Questions asked:
1. Detect peak in a array of heights
2. Stack all 1s in an  array of 0 and 1 irrespective of position of clubed element.
3. 2 quesitons based on BFS and DFS cobined with DP so for each stage you have to maintain some maxima possible till the previous stage, thats a basic DP just combined with BFS and DFS.
4. time complexity question-
