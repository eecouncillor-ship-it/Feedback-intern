# Group Discussion (GD) Questions
**Company:** IMC
**Profile:** Quantitative Research

We were given a problem statement and asked to work on it individually for 20
minutes. Then we were split into groups of 4 to discuss everyone's ideas and the
potential weaknesses of the solutions. In the end we had to come up with a final
solution taking everyone's ideas into account and present it to the panel.

**Problem statement:** Given football match data consisting of team A, team B, the
11 players in each team, red cards, yellow cards, goals scored by each team, the
percentage of time the ball was with each team, home/away for each team etc., come
up with an algorithm to predict the winner of a new football match. No black box
models - you need to have a rationale for everything you propose.

**Tips**
- Be confident. They will 100% notice and it will help in future rounds.
- Make valid points, and if you have nothing to add, summarise whatever has been
  discussed so far and redirect the conversation in your favour.
- Own up to your mistakes and NEVER double down on a mistake.
- Be a good listener and do not try to one-up other candidates just for the sake
  of it.
