# Interview Questions
**Company:** IMC
**Profile:** Quantitative Research

**Result:** Rejected.

**Technical Interview 1**
- Given a new alphabet with x vowels and y consonants, each question asked for the
  number of ways to construct a word matching certain conditions. Basic PnC ideas
  like stars and bars, picking with/without replacement and inclusion-exclusion
  were tested.
- Puzzle: you are given an n x n board and a T-shaped tile made of 3 horizontal
  squares and one square attached below the middle square. Find the number of ways
  to place this tile on the board. Follow up: if you can add new squares to either
  the left/right end or below the centre squares (as many times as you want), how
  many total ways are there to fit the tile on the board? (You need to find the sum
  of the ways over all types of tiles constructed this way.)

**Technical Interview 2**
- Question 1 was the same as question 2 but without the friend.
- Question 2: there is a car dealership where you can get good cars and bad cars
  with probability 0.3 and 0.7. I have a friend who can predict if a car is good or
  bad with accuracy 0.9. I can only know the quality of the car once I have bought
  it. The value of a good car to me is 10000 and a bad car is 5000; the cost to the
  seller is 7000 and 3000 respectively. I need to maximise utility, given as
  value - how much I paid.

**General tips - things they do on purpose**
- "Are you willing to bet your interview on the answer", 3 times per interview.
- A 10 second blank stare before saying ok and moving on to the next question.
- Interrupting you while you solve a question.

Don't be fazed by any of this. They do it to everyone and it is just their way of
checking whether you will break under pressure and how confident you are in your
answers.
