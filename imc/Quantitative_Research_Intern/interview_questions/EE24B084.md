# Interview Questions
**Company:** IMC
**Profile:** Quantitative Research Intern

3 rounds, 
First was pnc based. Questions along the lines of how many words can be formed with certain letters and certain combinatoric constraints. Some pnc based puzzles.

Second was a probab based interview, went over some scenario of utility and best bids for certain scenarios. The example they used was buying a car from a dealership where the car has certain probab of being good and certain probab of being bad. The continuation was being given extra information from a test drive of the car. And now further questions along the lines of how much can I pay for the test drive.
Then some poisson distribution based questions, followed by some general probab questions.

Third was a behavioural round.
