# Interview Questions
**Company:** Zanskar Research LLP
**Profile:** Quantitative Analyst

**Result:** Rejected.

**Interview 1**
- Question 1: you are at position 0 of a number line (-inf to inf) and move to
  position 1 with probability 1. At every position other than 0 you move with
  probability 1/2 to the right and 1/2 to the left. What is the probability you
  eventually reach zero again?
- Question 2: there are N cars each with a distinct speed. If a fast car is behind
  a slow car then the fast car will travel at the speed of the slower car when it
  reaches it, and will become part of its cluster. What is the expected number of
  clusters?
- Question 3: if P is a prime >= 3, prove that P^2 - 1 is divisible by 24.
- Some more probability questions, no puzzles.
