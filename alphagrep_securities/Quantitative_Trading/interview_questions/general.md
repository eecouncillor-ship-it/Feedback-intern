# Interview Questions
**Company:** AlphaGrep Securities
**Profile:** Quantitative Trading

2 probability questions and 2 algorithmic questions, then a light HR round.

**Probability 1:** Given a stick, you break it into 3 pieces. What is the
probability that it forms a triangle? (Directly from Greenbook.)

**Probability 2:** A monkey rolls a die. If he rolls 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 he collects
the corresponding amount; if he obtains a 6 he collects 6 and rolls again. What is
the expected value of the amount collected by the monkey?

**Algorithmic 1:** You have a row of houses and 3 colours with which you can paint
them - red, green or blue - on the condition that adjacent houses cannot be the
same colour. Given the corresponding costs for painting each house each colour,
what is the minimum amount you can spend to paint all the houses?

**Algorithmic 2:** You are given a string of lowercase letters. Using all the
letters, how many palindromic strings can you construct?

**Advice**
- Algorithmic 1 is very straightforward DP. The interviewer wanted me to explain
  why DP and why not greedy (why would it fail, and can you give a failing
  example?). He also wanted a dry run for the example he gave, and pseudocode. So
  more than identifying that it was DP, he wanted to know why other approaches
  would fail. It was also important to mention overlapping subproblems.
- Algorithmic 2: very important to mention recursion and show how the algorithm
  would work (symmetry around the centre of the string). Pseudocode was not asked
  for this one.

**Tips**
- Please do not be stressed before the interview.
- Have a physical copy of your resume with you.
