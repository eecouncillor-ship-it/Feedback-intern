# Interview Questions
**Company:** Pluswealth Capital Management LLP
**Profile:** Software Engineering

**Result:** Rejected.

**Interview 1**
- Question 1: you have a triangle with each vertex as a spot and 2 spots on each
  edge. How many ways are there to arrange the numbers 1-9 (all need to be used) on
  these spots such that the sum of numbers along any edge = 17 (including both
  vertices)?
- Question 2: you have x small biscuits and y large biscuits. You draw a biscuit at
  random; if it is large you break it into 2 small ones, eat one of them and place
  the other back. You continue this till the bag has no more large biscuits left.
  What is the expected number of small biscuits you eat?
- Question 3 (don't remember exactly) was related to linearity of expectation.
- C++ theory: virtual functions and vtables, casting operations on pointers and the
  result you get, how STL containers are implemented under the hood, etc.
