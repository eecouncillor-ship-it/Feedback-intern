# Interview Questions
**Company:** GlobalFoundries
**Profile:** DFT

There were 2 rounds of interview; 1 technical and 1 hr.
For the technical round first they asked first about CMOS and what would happen if you inverted the transistors of a CMOS inverter, and in what causes would both transistors be conducting.
Next they asked digital questions, 
Why is nand gate universal
Make a mux using nand gates
Make and gate using mux
Why would there be variations in propagation delay of two identical gates, that one delved into manufacturing side, I simply said we were not taught about it.
Then he asked to make an fsm for a certain function, relatively simple.

For the hr round he asked mainly about my course projects, digital and mup specifically, and why did I choose DFT and gf.
