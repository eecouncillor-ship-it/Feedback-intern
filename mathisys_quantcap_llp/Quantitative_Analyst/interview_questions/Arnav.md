# Interview Questions
**Company:** Mathisys Quantcap LLP
**Profile:** Quantitative Analyst

**Interview 1**
- You are given a hexagon with all sides coloured blue. You need to colour every
  diagonal red or blue such that if you select any three vertices, the triangle
  formed has at least one blue side. Find the number of valid colourings.
- 0 < x1 < x2 < x3 < x4 < x5 < 100, all xi integers. Find E[x4].
- There is a cube with one vertex at the origin. An ant is at the origin, and at
  any point it has an equal chance of moving along any edge originating from its
  current position. Find the number of paths for which the ant is at the origin
  after t = 6, and also solve for general t.
- Optimisation of LIS from O(n^2) to O(n log n).

**Interview 2**
- Gale-Shapley algorithm.
- Huffman coding.
- 200 noodles (both ends free) in a bowl. At every step you take two ends and tie a
  knot; repeat till no ends are left. What is the expected number of loops formed?
- Number of ways to fill an n x n matrix with zeros and ones such that the sum of
  each row and each column is zero.
- A tosses 20 coins, B tosses 21. The player with more heads wins. What is the
  probability of each player winning?
- Open ended: an airline has only k seats available, but passengers keep arriving
  one by one and the airline doesn't know in advance how many passengers will
  eventually arrive. Initially, the first k passengers are seated. After that,
  whenever a new passenger arrives, the airline is allowed either to reject them or
  to seat them by ejecting one of the currently seated passengers. Design an
  algorithm so that after all passengers have arrived, every passenger who ever
  arrived has exactly the same probability of occupying a seat. (Reservoir
  sampling.)
