# Interview Questions
**Company:** Mathisys Quantcap LLP
**Profile:** Quantitative Analyst

TECHNICAL ROUND - 1

1) You are given a hexagon whose all 6 sides are coloured Blue. Now you are to colour all of its diagonals either Red or Blue, such that no triangle formed by the sides & diagonals has all its sides Red coloured. What is the number of ways to do such a colouring?
2) A & B play a best of 3 game and say the probability A wins a individual match is x. Under what condition will you bet that the winner is determined exactly after 2 matches? Say you win $b if the winner is determined exactly in 2 matches, for what values of b will you do do this betting for $1?
3) You randomly choose 5 distinct integers between 0 and 100 (exclusive). What is the expected value of the second smallest of the 5 numbers?

TECHNICAL ROUND - 2

1)  Find the longest increasing subsequence in an array of n elements. Bonus: Can you do it in O(nlogn) time.
2) Say you have a circular necklace, and you place 3 beads on the necklace on the circumfence uniformly random. Say, one of those 3 beads is gold coloured bead, what is the expected length of the arc with the golden bead? What if you had n beads (of which 1 is gold)? Why is this not just 2/n times circumfence?
3) Say a knockout tournament with 64 teams is about to begin, and you are gonna guess the winning team for all the 63 games in the tournament. You win $1 for a Round of 64 game, $2 for a Round of 32 game, and so on ..... Finals you win $32 for correctly guessing the winner of the game. What is your total expected winning?
4) Say there is a random prize generator machine which gives integer between 1-200 (inclusive), you can choose to put $1 and generate another prize or just walk away with the current prize. What is your strategy to get maximum prize? and what is that expected maximum prize?
