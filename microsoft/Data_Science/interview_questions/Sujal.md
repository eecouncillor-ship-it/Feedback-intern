# Interview Questions
**Company:** Microsoft
**Profile:** Data Science

**Result:** Rejected after round 1.
(Keerthi was also shortlisted but did not attend round 2 - was in another interview.)

**Round 1**
He touched on every topic - started with the covariance matrix, variance, bias.
- Started with very basic things like classification models.
- CNNs, not so deep.
- Questions about transformers and how they work: what is attention, what is the
  difference between transformers and RNNs.
- Classification model metrics - ROC-AUC, confusion matrix.
- Why is there a curve in ROC-AUC when we are plotting for individual values?
- An easy DSA question.
- He asked me to write pseudocode for SVM, just to check whether we know the
  process to build a model.
- Was asked to code up XGBoost from scratch, because I had written it on my resume.

I don't remember the other questions, but the interviewer was chill. He tried to
check the thought process rather than a full-fledged answer. The interview was
mostly about AI/ML concepts covered in our courses. The interviewer was very sweet
and gave constant hints throughout.
