# Interview Questions
**Company:** Microsoft
**Profile:** Software Engineering

**Result:** Rejected.

**Interview 1**
A pretty hard DSA question. I don't remember the exact question, but I remember
asking the interviewer openly for hints. I didn't have a good experience with
Microsoft - they didn't join the meeting on time and kept calling me while I was
giving JPMC's interviews, and it kept getting delayed. I ended up giving my round
1 interview at 11:45 when they had already given all of their offers. The
interviewer couldn't care less and he solved the question for me.

**Interview 2**
I was asked to geek out about my resume, and she especially took interest in the
Jack compiler I made as part of DA2304 and wanted me to elaborate on it. Later, for
about 20 minutes, I was asked to code up min path sum of a binary tree. I misread
the question and solved for something else - make sure you don't make the same
mistake and read every question once or twice. I wasn't guided back in the right
direction till the end of the interview, after which I didn't have time to code
the right approach. Overall it didn't go well for me; this was right after JPMC
rejected me from HR so I was kind of let down.

General fundae: keep your head up no matter what happens, don't let one bad
interview mess up the rest of the interviews lined up for the day.
