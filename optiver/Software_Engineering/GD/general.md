# Group Discussion (GD) Questions
**Company:** Optiver
**Profile:** Software Engineering

66 shortlisted, 60 min. Groups of 5 - solve several variants of a trading puzzle by
discussing. 3 puzzles had to be solved together and 1 individually (plus a follow
up).

- Given initial amounts of a good and conversion rates to other goods, find the
  minimum number of trades to reach the final amount of a good. Drawing a graph
  makes the problem very simple. Note that at each point the amount of any good we
  are holding should be an integer value.
- Follow up: find the number of trades for 1 million units of the final good - use
  approximations for this.

**Tips**
- Make sure you speak up and that your contributions don't get drowned out in what
  others are saying. Take the initiative to explain the final answer and be
  confident.
- Strategise things beforehand with your group if you can talk to each other. We
  ended up fighting within our own group, causing conflicts and essentially
  lowering all our chances. (Sujal)
