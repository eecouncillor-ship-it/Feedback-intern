# Interview Questions
**Company:** Uber
**Profile:** Software Engineering

**Result:** Rejected.

**Round 1**
One DSA question: given a tree or graph with weights/distances, place a checkpost
on one of the nodes such that the sum of distances from that checkpost to all
other nodes is minimised.

**Round 2**
One DSA question: given a graph, find the length of the path in which all the
numbers (weights) are in increasing order, plus two other conditions. It can be
solved using DP or DFS. With DFS, first filter out the edges whose weights are not
in increasing order and then directly apply the remaining two conditions. Those
two conditions were largely similar to my round 1 question.
