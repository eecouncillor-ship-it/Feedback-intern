# Interview Questions
**Company:** Uber
**Profile:** Software Engineering

**Result:** Received offer (off-campus, via Uber She++).

Around the beginning of semester 4, Uber will notify about a 2-day networking event
called Uber She++ (for women and LGBTQ+, I believe). It had an online assessment
and candidates were shortlisted based on their performance in the OA (63 candidates
in India were selected). All expenses paid travel and accommodation, and 2 days of
events organised by Uber at their Bangalore office.

Before the campus internship drive they reach out to She++ candidates to organise
2 technical interviews and extend internship offers to those who qualify. This is
an easy way out of the campus internship drive for those who meet the above
criteria.

- Question 1: Find the Safest Path in a Grid (LeetCode).
- Question 2: A greedy question which used priority queues and involved positions
  of gas tanks, refill amounts, and initial and end positions. What are the minimum
  refills required? Follow up: each refill i takes Ti time - what is the minimum
  time required?
