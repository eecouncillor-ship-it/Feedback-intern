# Interview Questions
**Company:** Goldman Sachs
**Profile:** Quantitative Trading

**Result:** Received offer (accepted).

**Interview 1**
- Asked about a few points on my resume.
- Generic probability questions and puzzles you can find on any website.
- Finance questions (only if you mention that you have knowledge of finance):
  calculate the value of yield given market price, principal, coupon and time to
  maturity, etc.

**Interview 2**
- Again, some resume questions.
- Generic probability questions using the uniform distribution, Markov chains and
  gambler's ruin.
- Finance questions based on option greeks: how to find the first derivative, the
  second derivative, how to speed up computation - i.e. what optimisations can we
  make while computing the derivatives.

**Interview 3**
- Given a scenario, construct the Markov chain for it.
- If the probability of getting heads on the i-th flip of a coin is 1/(2i + 1),
  what is the probability of getting an even number of heads?
- Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock (LeetCode).
  - Follow up 1: we can execute >= 1 trades and cannot hold more than 1 unit of a
    stock at once.
  - Follow up 2: there is a transaction cost incurred with every trade (1 buy +
    1 sell makes 1 trade).
