# Test Questions
**Company:** JPMorgan Chase
**Profile:** Data and AI

90 min total.

**30 min** - around 30 questions across 3 sections: probability, general maths
(linear algebra) and computer science (C++ and Python questions).

**60 min** - 2 DSA questions:
1. Very easy - find the maximum value of k (radius) we have to select such that
   every sprinkler can spray water to every tree (1D coordinates of sprinklers
   and trees were given). Here k is the maximum distance of a tree from a
   sprinkler at which it can still receive water.
2. A 2D DP problem - find a robot's maximum initial energy for it to move through
   a 2D grid from start to finish while maintaining positive energy, where every
   grid cell has either a positive or a negative value that changes the robot's
   current energy accordingly.

I heard other people got harder DSA questions which were graph related.
