# Interview Questions
**Company:** Texas Instruments
**Profile:** Digital

1. Resume questions
2. Qs on analog course project. How do you design a bandpass? What exactly are you doing to get peak voltage to feed it to LED driving logic?
3. Design freq/3 circuit. How do u make it 50 percent duty cycle. Then design freq/5. 
4. Write verilog code for freq/5 circuit with 50% duty cycle.
5. They gave a verilog code and asked what is wrong (it was a sequential always block with two if statements if (a) and if(b))(problem was that there was a latching issue because there was no case considered when neither a nor b occurs)
6. What is synchronous and asynchronous resest. How will u make an asynchronous reset into synchronous. How will u take care of reset de-assertion issue
7. How will u detect a posedge of clock or any signal in common
8. C programming (only logic was enough). Best way to find if number is power of 2 (do -1 and AND them). How will u toggle a particular bit in a number
9. What are setup and hold time. Why do they exist. Asked for maximum frequency of operation in a circuit they gave (the circuit had 2 asynchronous counters with combinational logic in between and also the second FF feeds back to the first (feedback) with another combinational logic)
10. Aptitude question: You have 8 balls and a weighing machine, and one of them is heavier than the rest. How many minimum measurements should you do to find the heavy ball
